02 — HEALTHCARE ARCHITECTURE

Better spaces.
Healthier lives.

Healthcare facilities are among the most demanding building types to plan. Clinical workflows, patient safety, accessibility, engineering systems, regulatory requirements and human experience must work together.

My focus is on making complex planning decisions clearer — connecting evidence, standards and design thinking from early programming through project delivery.

  • Functional planning & space programming
  • Standards-aware design and compliance review
  • Patient, staff and operational experience
  • Clearer workflows for architects and project teams
